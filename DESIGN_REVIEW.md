# DESIGN REVIEW — Échale (UI/UX pass)

## Honest critique (senior product design perspective)

### What already works
- Warm and culturally relevant visual direction (rosa mexicano + human stories).
- Strong product narrative and useful IA: discover → story → support flow.
- Core donation interaction is understandable and generally frictionless.

### Highest-impact issues holding it back from “judge-winning” quality
1. **Visual hierarchy is inconsistent**
   - Headline styles, body sizes, and spacing vary too much between sections.
   - Some paragraphs run too wide, reducing readability and perceived polish.

2. **Header feels overloaded and product-incoherent**
   - Heart/bell/profile implied features that do not exist.
   - The colorful strip under the header looked decorative but noisy and competed with core content.

3. **Home lacked a singular “hero moment”**
   - Good message, but not yet an iconic first impression with a strong visual anchor and clear dual CTA hierarchy.

4. **Card system had uneven density**
   - Story cards mixed decoration and data with slightly cramped rhythm.
   - Progress and trust signals needed cleaner visual grouping.

5. **Story page had too many competing surfaces**
   - Important actions (donate/share/progress/trust) were split across many blocks.
   - Desktop needed a coherent sticky donation rail instead of dispersed CTAs.

6. **Micro-interaction quality was uneven**
   - Some transitions felt nice, but states (pending/confirmed/waiting) needed stronger, clearer visual language.

7. **Trust language and support context needed stronger framing**
   - “Organización aliada” appears, but should be surfaced more deliberately as a confidence pattern.

8. **Minor “product smell” from dead or ambiguous actions**
   - Placeholder-style actions (e.g., “Ver más”) reduced credibility in a judged demo.

## Design goals for this pass
- Premium restraint: cleaner hierarchy, tighter palette, fewer decorative elements.
- Strong first 5-second impression on home.
- Story page optimized for conversion and trust.
- Every clickable action real and meaningful.
- Mobile polish at hackathon demo quality (390px).
